# Sources: Jazz Chord Building & Improvisation Repertoire (Free Only)

Free-only coverage across chord construction theory, voicing technique, repertoire strategy, free PDFs, and auditable courses.

## Sources

- [The Jazz Piano Site — Jazz Chord Voicings](https://www.thejazzpianosite.com/jazz-piano-lessons/jazz-chord-voicings/) — **tutorial** · Systematic free lesson series: voicing types, combination rules, application
- [JazzAdvice — Blueprint for Building Your Repertoire](https://www.jazzadvice.com/lessons/a-blueprint-for-building-your-repertoire/) — **article** · Concrete step-by-step strategy for selecting and internalizing standards
- [JazzAdvice — Jazz Improvisation Ultimate Guide](https://www.jazzadvice.com/lessons/jazz-improvisation-ultimate-guide/) — **article** · Comprehensive free guide linking ear training, language, and chord-scale practice
- [Open Music Theory v2](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/1254) — **book** · Free open undergraduate textbook covering jazz harmony and voice leading
- [Jamey Aebersold Free Scale Syllabus (PDF)](https://www.jazzbooks.com/mm5/download/FREE-scale-syllabus.pdf) — **primary** · Authoritative chord-scale reference PDF from the canonical jazz education series
- [JazzImproviser.com — PDF Downloads](https://jazzimproviser.com/pdf-downloads/) — **reference** · Free theory-focused PDFs on modern harmony, chord-scale application, improvisation
- [Coursera — Jazz Improvisation (Berklee / Gary Burton)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/jazz-improvisation) — **docs** · Full Berklee course auditable free; covers repertoire building and improv frameworks

## Search angles

- Chord construction theory (stacking thirds, extensions, alterations)
- Chord voicing technique (drop voicings, shell voicings, upper structures)
- Repertoire building strategy (standard selection, internalization process)
- Free PDFs and downloadable reference materials
