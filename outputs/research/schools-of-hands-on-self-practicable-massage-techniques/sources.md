# Sources: schools of hands-on, self-practicable massage techniques

Curated links on massage/bodywork schools that a person can practice on themselves — sweeping the modality overview, self trigger-point and myofascial work, reflexology/acupressure, Ayurvedic Abhyanga, Thai Reusi Dat Ton, plus the clinical evidence and its limits.

## Sources

### reference

- [Types of Massage Therapy and Bodywork](https://mblexguide.com/types-of-massage-therapy-and-bodywork/) — **reference** · Professional taxonomy mapping the major schools across 350+ modalities.
- [Reflexology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflexology) — **reference** · Canonical encyclopedia entry on the reflexology school and its claims.
- [Foot Reflexology Chart: Points, How-To, Benefits, Risks (Healthline)](https://www.healthline.com/health/foot-reflexology-chart) — **reference** · Medically-reviewed self-reflexology point map with honest risk notes.

### tutorial

- [A step-by-step guide to giving yourself a massage (PopSci)](https://www.popsci.com/story/health/trigger-point-self-massage-how-to/) — **tutorial** · Plain step-by-step walkthrough of self trigger-point massage.
- [How to release trigger points yourself (Dr Graeme)](https://www.drgraeme.com/articles/2022/02/how-to-release-treat-trigger-points-yourself) — **tutorial** · Dedicated educator's method for self-applied trigger-point release.
- [Self-Myofascial Release / Trigger Point Therapy (TotalMSK)](https://www.totalmsk.co.uk/msk-blog/selftreatment/trigger-point-therapy-smr.html) — **tutorial** · Self-myofascial release technique from a musculoskeletal clinic.
- [Ayurvedic Self-Oil Massage — Abhyanga (Banyan Botanicals)](https://www.banyanbotanicals.com/pages/ayurvedic-self-oil-massage) — **tutorial** · Authoritative head-to-toe Abhyanga self-massage sequence.
- [Reusi Dat Ton — ITTM's 15 Exercises (Traditional Bodywork)](https://www.traditionalbodywork.com/reusi-dat-ton-ittms-15-exercises-ecourse/) — **tutorial** · Thai self-massage/joint-mobilization lineage, the root system of Thai massage.

### article

- [Trigger Point Massage: What Physical Therapists Want You to Know (Hinge Health)](https://www.hingehealth.com/resources/articles/trigger-point-massage/) — **article** · Physiotherapist framing of safe self trigger-point work.
- [Self-massage therapy (AIA)](https://www.aia.com/en/health-wellness/healthy-living/healthy-body/Self-massage-therapy) — **article** · General self-massage wellness primer from a health insurer.
- [Study finds limited evidence for massage therapy's effectiveness (News-Medical)](https://www.news-medical.net/news/20240721/Study-finds-limited-evidence-for-massage-therapys-effectiveness-in-pain-relief.aspx) — **article** · Accessible summary of the skeptical evidence picture.

### paper

- [A Meta-Analysis of Foam Rolling on Performance and Recovery (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6465761/) — **paper** · Quantifies what self-myofascial release (foam rolling) actually does.
- [Foam Rolling Training Effects on Range of Motion: Systematic Review (Springer)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-022-01699-8) — **paper** · Best-evidence synthesis on SMFR and flexibility gains.
- [Evidence Map of Massage Therapy for Painful Conditions, 2018–2023 (NCBI)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK606161/) — **paper** · VA evidence map; where massage has support and where it doesn't.

### book

- [Hand and Foot Reflexology: A Self-Help Guide (AbeBooks)](https://www.abebooks.com/9780133835892/Hand-Foot-Reflexology-Self-Help-Guide-0133835898/plp) — **book** · Full self-practice manual for the reflexology school.

## Search angles

- Overview / canonical — taxonomy of massage schools and modalities (MBLEx guide, Wikipedia).
- Self trigger-point & myofascial — self-applied pressure and SMFR technique (PopSci, Dr Graeme, TotalMSK, Hinge Health).
- Reflexology / acupressure self-practice — foot/hand point work (Healthline, self-help book).
- Ayurvedic tradition — Abhyanga self-oil massage (Banyan Botanicals).
- Thai tradition — Reusi Dat Ton self-massage and joint mobilization (Traditional Bodywork).
- Clinical evidence & critique — what holds up and what doesn't (PMC, Springer, NCBI evidence map, News-Medical).

Notes / gaps: I read the topic's "self-practicalbe" as **self-practicable** (techniques you can do on yourself). A clean Do-In / self-shiatsu source did not surface above content-farm quality, so that branch of the Japanese tradition is under-covered here. Much of the practical self-massage material is vendor- or wellness-blog-hosted rather than peer-reviewed — the `paper` entries are there to keep the set honest about the thin clinical evidence.
